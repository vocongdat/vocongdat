## 🚀 About Me

For over four years I've shipped production web apps across startups, enterprise and freelance work — from a **SURF 2025 award-winning** tourism platform to NFT marketplaces, banking procurement systems and blockchain games. I've grown from writing pixel-perfect landing pages into **architecting front-end systems** and **leading teams** of up to 100.

My sweet spot is the seam between Figma and the browser: component-driven architecture (Atomic Design, Storybook), thoughtful state management, and relentless attention to Core Web Vitals and SEO with Next.js SSR/SSG. Lately I lean on AI-augmented workflows to ship faster without lowering the bar.

- 🎓 Computer Engineering — Da Nang University of Science and Technology (DUT)
- 🎯 Focus: Performance · DX · UI craft
- ✈️ 🍲 Beyond the code: traveler and food lover — new places, cultures and history keep inspiring how I build for people.
